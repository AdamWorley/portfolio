---
title: Away From The Keyboard
---

Indoor rock climbing is my go-to. It's a physical problem and a mental one at
the same time, which is most of why it holds my attention.

Running does a similar job at a different pace. The rhythm of a long run is
where a busy week finally goes quiet.

Badminton when I want a friendly match, and stand-up paddleboarding when what I
want is flat water and no noise at all.

Otherwise it's films, games, and working through a decent coffee.
